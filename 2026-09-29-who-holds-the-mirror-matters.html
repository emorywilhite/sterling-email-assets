Who holds the mirror matters

Ask your own people what they see.

A lot of it will match what you already believe. They use your words. They sit in your meetings.

Would an independent team see the same organization you see?

That's The Mirror Test. One version is what you intend. The other is what people experience, and what the results prove. Who looks decides which version you get back.

They don't work for you. The Board draws people from every kind of organization. They train on one standard. Then they use it on a place that isn't theirs.

Your team shares the first version. This class can see the second.
