THE BLIND SPOT

 

The most misunderstood step in the 2027 cycle

I have sat across from a lot of leaders on Visionary Voices, and the honest ones almost all say a version of the same thing. Something in the organization is not working, and nobody inside it can say exactly what.

These are not leaders sitting still. The retreats happen. The consultants come and go. The new plans pile up. And the feeling stays. You don’t need another plan. You need a system.

The Sterling Assessment is that system. It compares your organization to the Framework for Performance Excellence, all seven areas, from Leadership to Results, and tells you in plain terms what is working and what is not.

But before any of that comes a first step so small that leaders keep getting it wrong. It is called the Application of Intent, and it is due this month.

Here is what it is. You tell Sterling that your organization wants to look into the 2027 cycle. That is the whole step.

Here is what it is not. It is not the full application. You do not send proof of anything. You are not signing up for the whole cycle. The full application is not due until November, and what you do between now and then is up to you.

I have watched a lot of leaders wait for the year they finally feel ready. The ones who grow fastest do it the other way around. They raise their hand first and get ready after.

The door is open through the end of August. That is about four weeks from today.

Start your 2027 cycle

The Application of Intent is due this month. The full application is not due until November.
