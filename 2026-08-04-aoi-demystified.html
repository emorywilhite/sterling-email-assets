THIS WEEK

 

The most misunderstood step in the 2027 cycle

I have spent enough hours across from leaders on Visionary Voices to notice a pattern. The honest ones all circle the same suspicion: something in the organization is off, and nobody inside it can quite name it.

The improvement efforts are real. The retreats happen, the consultants come and go, the initiatives stack up. And the suspicion stays. You don’t need another initiative. You need a system.

Naming what is off is exactly what the Sterling Assessment was built to do. It measures an organization against the Framework for Performance Excellence, all seven categories, Leadership through Results, and hands you back language for the thing you could not name.

But before any of that, there is a first step so small that leaders keep misreading it. It is called the Application of Intent, and it is due this month.

Here is what it is. A declaration. You are telling Sterling that your organization intends to explore the 2027 cycle. That is the whole step.

Here is what it is not. It is not the application. It is not a submission of evidence. It does not obligate you to go further. The full application is not due until November, and the distance between the two is yours.

I have watched a lot of leaders wait for the year when they will feel ready. The ones who grow fastest do it in the other order. They raise their hand first and get ready second.

The door for declaring is open through August. That is this month, and this month has a habit of ending.

Start your 2027 cycle

The Application of Intent is due this month. The full application is not due until November.

Bob Madeiros
Co-Host, Visionary Voices
